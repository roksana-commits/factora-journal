# Factora Journal — Homepage Content Plan

## Language and audience

Primary interface language: English, matching the international reference-journal context. Copy will be concise, source-conscious and readable for general audiences. The design itself will remain culturally neutral while the first issue highlights several South Asian subjects.

## Homepage sequence

| Order | Section | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Research ticker + masthead | Establish recency, journal identity and primary navigation |
| 2 | Asymmetric hero | State the evidence-first promise and expose methodology links |
| 3 | Category index | Let visitors browse Schools, Universities, People, Organizations, Places, Events/Documents and Creative Works |
| 4 | Featured dossier | Present one visually dominant profile with source-status metadata |
| 5 | Recent research stream | Show varied entities in an editorial list rather than repetitive cards |
| 6 | Evidence standard | Explain research, source labels, open questions and corrections |
| 7 | Dispatch signup | Invite a monthly research digest without aggressive marketing |
| 8 | Editorial footer | Repeat browse links, policies, contact and independence statement |

## Editorial samples

The initial static edition will use concise, non-exhaustive sample summaries for: **The Meghna Delta**, **Begum Rokeya**, **Bangladesh National Museum**, **University of Dhaka**, **Dhaka Metro Rail**, and **Ekushey Book Fair**. The interface will avoid invented source counts and will label the collection as a demonstration edition. Links will scroll to relevant homepage sections or show a clear “demo edition” notification rather than leading to dead ends.

## Functional scope

Mobile navigation, category filtering, search overlay, newsletter-form feedback, theme-safe contrast, focus states, reduced-motion support and smooth section navigation will be implemented entirely on the client. No database, account system or external API is required.
