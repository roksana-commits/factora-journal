# Factora Journal — Neutral Content and SEO Architecture

The publication will target English-speaking audiences in the **United States and Europe** without presenting any school, university, place, person, organization, event, document or creative work as a finished profile. Until editorial files are ready, the homepage will present only the collection taxonomy, research workflow, publication standard and collection-readiness states.

| Surface | Approved content model | Excluded content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Independent, source-conscious reference publishing | Regional references and named entities |
| Collection index | Generic categories and research purpose | Named schools, universities, places or profiles |
| Featured section | “Before a profile is published” methodology dossier | Any sample entity presented as researched |
| Research stream | Collection readiness and editorial status | Source counts, update claims and sample profile titles |
| Search | Category, policy and methodology destinations | Searches over nonexistent profiles |

The canonical production origin is `https://factorajnl-wsynrwoe.manus.space`. Every indexable route will receive a unique title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph URL and page-level structured data. The crawl layer will include a single canonical sitemap, explicit search and AI crawler rules, `llms.txt`, and a fuller machine-readable editorial overview in `llms-full.txt`.
