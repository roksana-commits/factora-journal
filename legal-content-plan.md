# Factora Journal — Legal and Contact Content Structure

The legal suite will be written for a **demonstration editorial website**, avoiding claims about services, data handling or commercial operations that the static project does not perform. Each page will show an effective date, a concise plain-language summary, anchored sections and a reminder that the copy should be reviewed before production use.

| Route | Page | Core sections |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/legal/privacy` | Privacy Policy | Information received, automatic technical data, purposes, sharing, retention, choices, children, changes, contact |
| `/legal/terms` | Terms of Use | Acceptance, editorial nature, permitted use, intellectual property, submissions, third-party links, disclaimers, liability, termination, governing framework, changes |
| `/legal/cookies` | Cookie Policy | Current cookie posture, essential storage, analytics configuration, third parties, browser controls, changes, contact |
| `/legal/disclaimer` | Disclaimer | Informational purpose, accuracy and updates, no professional advice, third-party sources, external links, independence, fair use, limitation |
| `/editorial-policy` | Editorial Policy | Independence, selection, verification, attribution, conflicts, updates, corrections |
| `/source-policy` | Source Policy | Source hierarchy, first-party labels, anonymous sources, archival material, link preservation, open questions |
| `/methodology` | Research Methodology | Scope, discovery, claim mapping, verification, writing, review, publication, ongoing maintenance |
| `/corrections` | Corrections Policy | Reporting an issue, review, correction levels, visible record, response expectations |
| `/about` | About Factora | Mission, demonstration status, editorial principles and independence |
| `/contact` | Contact Us | Editorial inquiries, corrections, rights/privacy requests, general messages and static form feedback |

The contact form will validate name, email, subject and message in the browser, then provide transparent confirmation that the **static demonstration does not transmit or store submissions**. A production integration can be added later.
